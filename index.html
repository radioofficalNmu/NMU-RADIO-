About The Station

More Than A Radio.
It's Our Voice.

NMU Radio is a student-focused online radio platform designed to connect members of the NMU community through music, conversation, information and entertainment.

Our vision is to create a platform where students can express themselves, discover talent, share ideas and stay connected with campus life.
